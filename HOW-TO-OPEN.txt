This website must be opened through a server. Double-clicking the files will NOT show images.

EASIEST (online link):
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the whole "cybernaut-site" folder onto that page.
4. You get a link like https://xxxx.netlify.app, and every page and image works.

ON YOUR COMPUTER:
1. Install Node.js (nodejs.org)
2. Open a terminal inside this folder and run:   npx serve .
3. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser
