THE MAN BEHIND CYBERNAUT

Building the Systems That Shape Tomorrow.

Every generation inherits a world. The next generation has the responsibility to redefine it.

Cybernaut was founded by Jaya Surya, with a simple belief: technology should not only solve problems, but create possibilities.

What started as a vision has grown into a mission to build intelligent systems, learning ecosystems, and opportunities that transform curiosity into capability, capability into confidence, and confidence into leadership.

For him, Cybernaut is not just a company.

It is a platform to build people, technology, and possibilities for tomorrow.

Because the future is not something to wait for.

It is something to build.

Jayasurya Gnanavel

Founder & CEO
The Mind Behind Cybernaut

Jayasurya Gnanavel is a philomath, technologist, and entrepreneur driven by curiosity and the belief that technology can shape people, businesses, and the future.

What began as a student club at SRM in 2021 has grown into Cybernaut, an ecosystem working with colleges, industry partners, and 75,000+ learners across India.

His journey is centred around one idea: build people who are capable of building the future.

He explores emerging and deep technologies, builds entrepreneurial ecosystems, mentors young minds, and creates platforms where students are encouraged to think independently, experiment boldly, lead with purpose, and build something of their own.

As a philomath, he constantly learns across disciplines, connects ideas, and turns curiosity into action.
