Course Overview

A complete path from
first line to shipped.

UI/UX Design teaches you to build products that work, not just products that look good. You'll start with the fundamentals of how users think, move through research and wireframing, and finish with high-fidelity Figma prototypes and a documented design system ready for handoff to developers.

The difference between a designer who gets hired and one who doesn't is usually the ability to explain their decisions. Every project in this program requires you to present your design rationale, not just your screens. By the end of Month 3, you'll have a portfolio that shows work and thinking.
