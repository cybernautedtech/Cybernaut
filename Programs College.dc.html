Centre of Excellence

Introducing
Cybernaut Campus.

Our Centre of Excellence model. A Cybernaut engineering lab, inside your college.

Most college partnerships are transactional. A vendor comes in, delivers a course, leaves. Cybernaut Campus is different. We embed a fully operational engineering lab inside your institution and run it like a product company runs an engineering team.

Your students don't attend classes in this lab. They report to work. Sprint boards, code reviews, mentor standups, production-grade workflows. For four years, they operate the way engineers at real companies operate. By the time placement season arrives, interviews aren't a test. They're a conversation about work they've already done.

The Model
01
Custom Lab, Calibrated to Your Intake

We assess your CS stream strength first: batch size, current skill baseline, placement history. Then we design the lab infrastructure and program depth around it. No template. No copy-paste deployment.

02
Weekly Lab Sessions, VAC-Integrated

Two structured lab sessions per week, woven into your academic calendar. Students practice inside the Cybernaut ecosystem: Coding Lab, LMS, and ERP. The same three tools our direct students use.

03
Industry Mentors On Rotation

Working engineers, not trainers reading slides, review student code, run sprint retrospectives, and hold students to the standard their first employer will.

04
4-Year Structured Roadmap, Sem 1 to Sem 8

Programming foundations in the first year. DSA and system design in the second. Projects and internships in the third. Full placement drive execution in the fourth. Every semester has a defined outcome, and your management gets a report against it.

05
Placement Drive Coordination Built In

We don't hand your students a certificate and wish them luck. We coordinate on-campus and off-campus drives, prep them per company, and track offer conversion, because that number is what your institution is ultimately measured on.

06
Full LMS Visibility for Your Administration

Your principal, HOD, and placement officer get a live dashboard: attendance, progress, at-risk students, project submissions. No monthly PDF reports. Real-time truth.
